The Climb

Tier I · The Flooded Foundations

The temple's flooded base: water, leeches, the first ranged weapon and the first boss.

“The ancients harnessed balance.”

K'in

Three floors at the foot of the pyramid, where the builders first held the elements in balance and the water never left. This is where you learn a run: choosing gates, keeping your health, and deciding when to stop.

T1A

The Drowned Gallery

WaterTiming

A tall flooded hall of dark green water. You cross stone islands and floating logs that drift between them, and one island has a band of spikes on a timer.

Deep water hides temple leeches. The shallow wading lane along the east wall is safe from them but slow, and Drowned Risen climb out of it as you pass, so the coward's route costs time and a fight. The golden idol sits on a pillar in the water.

T1B

The Sluice Hall

Key Orb roomDrain

The Key Orb sits on a pedestal at the bottom of a flooded channel, and a current pushes you back out.

Ride a stone lift to a balcony and press the plate there: the water drains through the sluice gate and the current stops. Take the orb and the room fights back, as three Risen Guardians rise in the galleries. The idol is on a column, reached from a crate.

Then the Portal Chamber, and up.

T1C

The Root Cellar

SwingSlope

A slick moss slope ends above a pit of tangled roots. Wooden platforms hang from vines and swing like pendulums across it, with a root island between them.

Cave bats swoop through the dark. Glowing fungus lights the way.

T1D

The Blowgun Shrine

Key Orb roomRanged

A square shrine with a raised altar that holds the Spirit Blowgun, the first ranged weapon.

Three round glyph targets sit high on the walls. Hit all three and the Key Orb rises from the altar, while cave bats harass the shooter.

Then a Spirit Shrine to rest, and the Portal Chamber.

Floor 3 · The Sump

New
Your first boss, your first Sun Gate
Enemies
The Mire Mother
Reward
Tier II Sun Gate

SUMP

The Mire Mother's Cistern

BossDrain

A vast round cistern with four sluice gates and four stone islands. The Mire Mother, an enormous glossy leech with glowing teal spots, slides under the water and lunges at your island.

Shoot the glyph targets on the gates to drain the water and leave her beached. Beat her, and the first Sun Gate lights, so your next run can start at Tier II.

Then the Portal Chamber, and up.
