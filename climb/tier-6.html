“A single orb chose memory over power.”

K'in

Everything you've learned is tested at once. The gauntlet asks for all four orbs on one route, the crucible throws every trap at you and gives your chisel its last test, and the Priest's Lieutenant guards the vault of the Sun Orb. Then you have to carry it out.

Every trap in the temple at once, in four quadrants: saws, spikes, darts and fire jets, and crushing walls, with timed doors between them. Recut what you can, and let them do your work.

T6D

The Lieutenant's Hall

Sun OrbBoss

The Priest's Lieutenant, an armoured warrior-priest with an eagle helmet and an obsidian spear, has ambushed you three times on the way up, and walked away each time. He guards the vault of the Sun Orb, and this time he doesn't leave.

The vault opens, and the Sun Orb is yours. Now you have to get it out.

LAST

The Last Breath

EscapeSun Orb

Carrying the Sun Orb is the heaviest thing you've done. You glow, and shade doesn't hide you. Every watcher in the temple is awake. There is no Waystall and no shrine, only one basin halfway. If you die, the orb goes back to its vault.

Everything comes back for one last climb: the long stair with a spirit behind you, a hall of giant faces, one last calendar, and a mirror walk. K'in stops speaking on this floor, for the first time since you met her.

Daylight, and the doors of the Sky Altar.

The Sun Orb

The brightest object in the game, resting in a cradle of four carved hands. You carry it up the last stair to the Sky Altar.
