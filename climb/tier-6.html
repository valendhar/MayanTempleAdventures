The Climb

Tier VI · The Ascension

The last two floors: every element and every trap, and the Sun Orb.

“A single orb chose memory over power.”

K'in

Everything Kaya has learned is tested at once. The gauntlet asks for all four orbs in turn, the crucible throws every trap at her together, and the Priest's Lieutenant guards the vault of the Sun Orb.

The Trap Crucible

Every trapTimed doors

Every trap in the temple at once, in four quadrants: saws, spikes, darts and fire jets, and crushing walls, with timed doors between them.

T6D

The Lieutenant's Hall

Sun OrbBoss

The Priest's Lieutenant, an armoured warrior-priest with an eagle helmet and an obsidian spear, guards the vault of the Sun Orb. Its exit leads to the summit.

The Lieutenant's vault opens onto the stair to the Sky Altar.

The Sun Orb

The brightest object in the game, resting in a cradle of four carved hands. Kaya carries it up the last stair to the Sky Altar.
