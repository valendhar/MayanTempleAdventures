The builders never used the grand stair: they had their own way down, hidden inside the walls, and they trapped every inch of it.

Six cracks in the temple's walls, each one visible only through the Lens. Behind them, the game changes shape: a side-on, one-screen-at-a-time trap platformer, running down through the pyramid to a hidden Sun Door that opens onto the Threshold.

They're the most dangerous way to walk home alive, and the richest. Finds carried out through a Way are worth half as much again, and they hold relics you can't get anywhere else.

View
Side-on, one fixed screen at a time
Lives
5 for the Way; one touch and you're dust
Tools
Staff poke, Ember Charges, ropes
Fair play
K'in warns once per trap and shows you what got you

How they play

One screen, one puzzle

A fixed camera shows one screen and cuts to the next when you cross the edge. Every trap is fully in view before you commit, so the second try feels clever, not lucky.

Learn the screen, beat it on the next try, and keep your lives for the screens that matter. If you run out, the Way throws you out, and you count as dead.

The toolkit

Handle the traps, don't just dodge them

Staff poke: trips wires and pressure stones from a safe distance. Ember Charges: break cracked blocks, but step away from the hiss. Ropes: unroll from bronze anchors to reach secret ledges.

The main path never needs a rope or a charge. They're for treasure, so using them is greed, never a dead end.
