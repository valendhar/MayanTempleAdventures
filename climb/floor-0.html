The Shell

The first level and the vertical slice: seventeen side-on screens, every base move taught once, and your first death.

The Shell is the first level, and the vertical slice: the half hour that has to prove the whole game. It is played side-on, in two rows of screens.

The lower row runs right, from the flooded atrium through the spike corridor, the fire gallery and the chasm. A shaft climbs. The upper row runs back left, to a bridge high over the atrium and the portal on top of the Sun Pillar, which glowed above you from the first second. It teaches every base move once, shows you two locks you can't open yet, and kills you once, on purpose, so that the first thing you really learn is the Return.

Seventeen screens, camp to portal

Nine rooms, two rows, one way up

In the order you meet them, from the flooded atrium to the portal on the Sun Pillar.

  1. 1

    The flooded atrium

    SpawnJumpSwim

    A spawn platform, stone islands in knee-deep water, and a pool to swim. The portal glows on top of the Sun Pillar far above you, so the goal of the floor is in view from its first second. Two things here are out of reach for now: a glyph target high over the pool, and a ledge too tall to climb.

    On a half-submerged step, Ix Nicte, Keeper of the First Step, stands dormant. K'in goes quiet near her.

  2. 2

    The spike corridor

    LensSun Shrine

    The first Sun Shrine stands at its door. Inside, a low corridor: three spike strips on different beats, and a stone serpent spitting fire from the far end. The Lens shows which plates are safe.

    The last plate kills you, as it kills Kaya in the short story. It is meant to happen. You wake at the Threshold, Oxlahun pours the tea, and there's a new face on the wall. When you come back, the temple has moved: the end door has dropped to a low slot, and you roll through it.

  3. 3

    The fire gallery

    TimingPush

    Five stepping stones over a pit of flame, with vents firing on a two-second beat. One block, one rule: push it into a vent's mouth, and that vent stays shut for good.

  4. 4

    The chasm

    Moving platformsFinds

    A still slab, a sliding one, a lifting one and a ferry, over a drop into the dark. A golden idol waits on a wall ledge. A fall costs a little health and puts you back on the platform you left.

  5. 5

    The plate door and the climb

    CarryLedge grab

    Carry a stone block onto the plate, and the door holds. A second Sun Shrine. Behind the door a shaft climbs four storeys on ledges, and a ladder at the top can be kicked down: the floor's first shortcut.

  6. 6

    The orb shrine

    Puzzle

    Three pedestals, gold, blue and red. Sun poured into water makes growth; fire is the decoy. Growth in the socket sends a vine across the floor that bursts open the way on.

  7. 7

    The beam switchback

    Toggle hazardTiming

    Two ramps climb back to the left, each crossed by a blue-white beam: one a plate turns off, one on a two-second cycle. Nothing is dodged here. It is walked, on the right half of the beat.

  8. 8

    The sword shrine

    CombatParry

    A high landing over the atrium. Kaya lifts the obsidian macuahuitl, and the first Risen Guardian steps out of its niche: the combo, the heavy strike, the roll, and the parry when its mark burns gold. Then two more Risen and a Novice.

  9. 9

    The bridge to the portal

    CutPogo

    Two hits cut the root curtain: the first lock the new blade opens. A bridge runs back over the atrium, broken in the middle, where spike urns stand on posts. Strike down on them and they throw you on. On the Sun Pillar: the portal, a third shrine, and a vine ladder down to the start.

Then home to the Threshold. Oxlahun's table opens, the Memory Wheel lights, and the middle portal leads up into the Flooded Foundations.
