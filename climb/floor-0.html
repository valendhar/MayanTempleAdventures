The Climb

The Shell

The tutorial floor: nine spaces on one route, and every core verb taught once.

The Shell was designed as one object before a single room was painted. The cutaway, the plan and the codex page all describe the same nine spaces and the single route through them.

The route spirals upward from a flooded atrium to a portal on top of the Sun Pillar, which stands in that same atrium. It is the tutorial: it teaches every core verb once, and nothing twice.

Place in the climb
The first floor, below Tier I
Rooms
Nine spaces on one route
Teaches
Every core verb once: move, time, think, fight
Enemies
One Risen Guardian
Reward
The obsidian macuahuitl, and the Threshold opens

One floor, three drawings

Nine rooms, one way up

In the order Kaya meets them, from the flooded atrium to the portal on the Sun Pillar.

  1. 1

    The entry hall

    SpawnMovement

    Where Kaya spawns. Wide, quiet, and lit by one shaft through the roof, so you can learn the camera and the jump with nothing at stake.

    On a half-submerged step, Ix Nicte, Keeper of the First Step, stands dormant.

  2. 2

    The fire-vent floor

    Timed hazardFixed beat

    Five stepping stones over a pit of flame. The first hazard, and the one that teaches you to read timing off the floor rather than off an enemy.

  3. 3

    The chasm

    FallPlatforming

    The floor runs out over a deep chasm. Sliding slabs and floating platforms carry you across, and not one of them waits for you. A golden idol glints on a ledge.

  4. 4

    The pressure plate

    PuzzleGate

    Stand on the carved plate and the door lifts. Step off and it drops. The first room that asks you to think about where your weight is.

  5. 5

    The trap corridor

    Layered hazardCorridor

    Two hazards on different rhythms in one narrow run: retractable spikes in the floor, and a stone serpent spitting fire from the end. Getting through once is luck; getting through clean means you have learned both beats.

    This is where Kaya dies in the short story.

  6. 6

    The orb shrine

    PuzzleShrine

    Orbs on pedestals, gold, blue and red, around a ring of glyphs, with one correct arrangement. K'in lights the orb that is wrong rather than the one that is right, so the solving is still yours.

  7. 7

    The beam switchback

    Toggle hazardTiming

    Blue-white energy beams cross a switchback ramp and switch on a toggle. Nothing is dodged here. It is walked, on the right half of the second.

  8. 8

    The sword shrine

    CombatReward

    Kaya lifts the obsidian macuahuitl off its pedestal, and the first guardian stands up. First real combat, on a high landing that overlooks the atrium.

  9. 9

    The bridge to the portal

    ExitNext floor

    A narrow bridge crosses high over the atrium to the round portal on top of the Sun Pillar. Step through and the Shell seals behind you, sword in hand.

Then the Portal Chamber, and up into the Flooded Foundations.
