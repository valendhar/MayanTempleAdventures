Two rooms appear on every floor of the climb. They are where it pauses: to rest, to bank what you carry, to hear K'in, and to breathe.

PC

The Portal Chamber

End of every floor

Every floor ends here. You set the Key Orb in the socket of carved cupped hands, a fragment of the mural lights, and K'in speaks one line. Take a rubbing of the fragment. Ring the brass bell, and a tray slides out of the wall with Oxlahun's voice behind it.

Then decide. Climb the three-step dais through the great round Portal Door and keep your Finds at risk. Pay to send them down the offering chute. Or take the small blue return portal and walk home alive.

SR

The Sun Shrine

Every floorCheckpoint

The checkpoint. A stone sun disc on a plinth that glows green-gold once it's lit. Resting at it refills Oxlahun's tea gourd, two sips that each heal a good part of your health, and saves your place. It also wakes the floor's ordinary enemies again, so rest or press on is a real choice.

Every floor has one or more, and one stands right before every boss door. A lit shrine on a cleared floor is also a start point from the Threshold: the way back to the pockets you couldn't open the first time.
