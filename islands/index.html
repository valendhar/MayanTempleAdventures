Mayan Temple Adventures

UEFN Islands

One temple, five Fortnite islands, all cut from the same rooms, traps and characters.

Mayan Temple Adventures is a family of five Fortnite islands, all set in and around the same living Mayan sun temple.

At the centre is the flagship, a co-op climb for up to four players through the Shell, seventeen floors and the Sky Altar. The other four islands take parts of that temple and turn them into their own genre: a deathrun through its traps, a tycoon at its foot, co-op escape rooms in its puzzle halls, and a raid on its elemental sanctums.

Who you are

You are a Climber

On every island you play as your own Fortnite character. Kaya, K'in and Oxlahun are the people you meet.

What they share

One temple, one set of rooms

Every island is cut from the same sealed rooms, traps and art. A room built once can serve a race, a dive, a puzzle chamber and the campaign.

How they connect

Each island stands alone

Each island keeps its own progress. They point to each other through the story, matching cosmetics and links to the flagship.

Five islands

Roadmap

Built in this order

Every phase ships something playable, and each one tests what the next one depends on. Everything before the flagship lowers its risk; everything after it reuses its rooms.

  1. Foundation

    The shared base every island is built on: one room standard, one library of traps, platforms and puzzle pieces, and party play for up to four from the start. Nothing is released yet.

  2. Island 1

    Trap Galleries ships first. It is quick to build, needs no combat, and shows how the temple's art, gap sizes and thumbnails land with real Fortnite players.

  3. Island 2

    The flagship opens in early access: the Threshold, the Shell with Kaya, and Floors 1 to 5. One tier follows per content update, with Trials opening tier by tier, until the Sky Altar.

  4. Island 3

    Sun Temple Dig, once the flagship is live: Isabel Kaan's abandoned camp as a tycoon, with real temple rooms as short dives.

  5. Island 4

    Illusion Depths, once the puzzle rooms of Tiers III and V exist in the flagship and can be recut for teams.

  6. Island 5

    Orb Raiders comes last, because it needs everything else: tuned combat, the four orb powers and the Tier IV sanctums. It is tested first as a weekend event.
