World

The Living Temple

One stepped pyramid, eight terraces high, that remembers everyone who climbs it.

The Sun Temple is not a ruin to be explored. It is a structure that remembers, reacts and makes every climb a test.

Its chambers shift to trap greedy intruders, its traps are physical and readable, and its motives remain a mystery. The only stable place inside is the Threshold, the base hall where every climb begins and every death returns.

Rules of the temple

  • Death inside the temple returns a climber to the Threshold, memories intact.
  • The temple keeps a record of every climber, on the mural and in the codex.
  • The chambers above shift; the Threshold never does.
  • The traps are physical: they can be read, timed and investigated.

Inside

A pyramid that climbs toward the sun

Six tiers stack inside the pyramid: flooded foundations, trap galleries, shifting vaults, four elemental sanctums, the illusion depths and a final ascension. Each tier has its own look, its own hazards and its own piece of the temple's history.

On the flat summit stands the small temple with the great Sun Portal, dark since the sealing. The climb, tier by tier →

The way in

The serpent-headed tunnel

Two enormous feathered serpents guard the entrance, their bodies carved up the first terrace, a sun mask on the lintel between them. A few worn steps lead down into the dark, toward a faint golden glow.

Outside

Jungle and camp

Dense jungle presses against the stone and hides the pyramid's base beneath vines and roots. At the foot of the pyramid, Kaya's camp is the last taste of ordinary life: a canvas tent, supply crates, a kettle ticking on the campfire.

The terraces

Eight steps to the sky

Eight sloped terraces with stepped cornices, jade bands and recessed sun panels rise to the summit temple, with the grand stair running up the front between serpent-headed balustrades.

Roots and moss weather the outside slowly; inside, the temple changes deliberately. Telling the two apart is part of reading the place.
