Warden of the Panels

Chak Tun

The only named guardian carved into the wall instead of standing in the hall, at the very edge of the panel that was re-cut.

A modest figure with a staff stands just outside the betrayal panel, his head turned back over his shoulder. He is the only figure on the wall who looks back.

In life he walked the length of the wall at dawn and dusk, looking for anything that had changed. He could say what the wall had looked like on any given day.

His name cartouche is cut short: the deeper recess of the neighbouring panel overruns the old border by two fingers' width and cuts straight through it. Under flat light the overrun is invisible. Under raking light it is the one record in the hall that tells the order of events without being asked.

“Nobody reads the margin. That is why someone has to stand in it.”

Chak Tun, in life

Design notes

His sheet shows the carving twice, under flat front light (how K'in lights it) and under low raking light (how Kaya reads it), plus a risen version that keeps the frozen posture: staff grounded, head forever turned back.
