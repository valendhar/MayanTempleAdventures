The trader

Oxlahun

The friendliest face in the Threshold, and its quietest threat. Called “Thirteen”, for the number of times he says he has died.

Oxlahun keeps the offering table: tea, supplies, a keepsake for every first death, and a tray of Spirit Fragments laid out like sweets.

He is a broad, easy man of no certain age, and the cleanest thing in a hall of damp stone and grey dust. He listens with his whole body, gets your name in the first minute, and has never once been wrong about a floor.

Every climber who returns tells him how they died, and he trades supplies for their Fragments and the gear they pawn before a hard climb. His help is real. So is the fact that his business only works while climbers keep dying.

Role
Merchant and soft antagonist
Found
The offering table in the Threshold
Signature
A disc of jade in one front tooth, embroidered red-and-indigo vest, spotless hands
Habit
Carves a tiny limestone keepsake of whatever killed you, “on the house”

“First one's on the house. Everybody gets one. Tell me where it got you.”

Oxlahun

Design notes

Bright, well-kept layers set him apart from the dusty hall at a glance: crimson sash, red-and-indigo embroidery, a canvas apron with a great many pockets. His hands are never idle, and when he is calculating they stop all at once; the stillness is his tell.
