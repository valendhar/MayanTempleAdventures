The surveyor

Mateo Vance

A broad-shouldered surveyor carved mid-measurement, one eye shut, sighting along a tall notched staff.

Mateo is the only carved climber shown working rather than standing, as if the survey were still in progress.

He brought professional tools into a building whose upper floors rearrange themselves to trap intruders. There is a cruel irony in a man whose craft depends on fixed points working in a pyramid built to move them.

His carving is the finest on the wall, so Kaya uses it as her comparison sample for tool marks. The seventeen notches on his staff may be graduations, or they may be tallies. A surveyor's unit and a captive's count of days look the same in stone.
