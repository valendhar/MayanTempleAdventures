The one who held the line

Lisbet

An older climber with a heavy coil of rope and an open hand reaching toward empty stone. She is almost smiling.

The rope's free end finishes in a neat loop, sized for a waist other than hers.

She came older and more experienced than the climbers Kaya imagines, with rope enough for two, which suggests she meant to bring someone through. No one is carved beside her.

The carver kept her laugh lines instead of smoothing them away. Lisbet breaks the pattern Kaya assumed about who the loop takes: she cannot be filed under greed.

“She was careful.”

K'in

Design notes

The only carved climber with a trace of a smile, and the only one whose open hand reaches toward nothing. The loop and the hand read together as an unfinished sentence.
