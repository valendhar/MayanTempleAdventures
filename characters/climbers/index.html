The Carved Climbers

The faces on the Spirit Mural: climbers who came before Kaya and never left.

One likeness for every climber K'in has lost, carved into the Spirit Mural so that no one goes unrecorded.

They exist only as reliefs, each about the length of a forearm, with dust settled into the grooves. Each sheet shows the carving twice: under flat front light, the way K'in lights it, and under low raking light, the way Kaya learns to read it. The newest figure on the strip is Kaya herself, still pale with fresh dust.
