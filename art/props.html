Art & Assets

Gameplay Props

Pads, plates, doors, traps and collectibles shared by every floor.

Everything Kaya can step on, pick up, solve or be hurt by: twenty-two sheets of shared gameplay props.

P01

Arrival pad

Round carved teleport pad: dormant, active and arriving

P02

Checkpoint stele

A sun-faced stele that lights when touched

P03

Exit gate

A corbel-arch gate whose slab sinks to reveal a golden veil

P04

Pressure plate set

Square, strip, heavy-weight and ground-pound plates; pressed and wrong

P05

Puzzle doors

A sliding relief door and an energy door with a jaguar-mouth orb socket

P06

Orb pedestals and sockets

Cupped-hand sockets, a jaguar-mouth wall socket and a glyph-ring pedestal

P07

Energy orbs

Gold sun, blue water, red fire and green growth; sun into water makes growth

P08

Key Orb

One per floor: a golden orb in a jade-and-gold cage that unfolds like petals

P09

Golden idols

Eight collectible seated figures, one hidden in every room

P10

Coins and Spirit Fragments

Bronze, silver and gold coins, and translucent golden soul shards

P11

Small collectibles

Codex pages, lore scrolls, glyph echoes, shards and trial medallions

P12

Stone platform kit

Static, sliding, floating and ferry platforms

P13

Floor spike trap

Obsidian-tipped spikes: retracted, warning and extended

P14

Serpent-head trap emitters

A fireball spitter, a fire jet and a dart head

P15

Fire pit and floor fire vents

A tiling fire pit, jaguar-mask vents and a vent ring

P16

Energy beam totems and seal barrier

A blue-white beam barrier, and a golden seal that dissolves

P17

Objective totem

Four glyph slots that light as a room's goals are met

P18

Glyph targets

Wall targets and a training post for the blowgun

P19

Crumbling floor tiles and stairs

Intact, triggered, collapsing and respawning

P20

Cracked breakable walls

Hinted with a green glow, shattered by the Earth Orb

P21

Boulders

A rolling boulder, a release chute, a pushable boulder and a weight plate

P22

Guardian niches and burial props

Niches, burial benches and sarcophagi where guardians rise
