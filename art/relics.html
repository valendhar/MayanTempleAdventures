Hero Items & Relics

Weapons, orbs, the spirit wisp and the field kit.

The objects the story turns on: the Climbers' weapons, the orbs, K'in, and the kit Kaya inherited from her mother.

How the weapons and powers play

W01

Obsidian Macuahuitl

The melee weapon: obsidian blades, gold bands and a jade glyph strip

W02

Spirit Blowgun

The first ranged weapon, with a jade hummingbird mouthpiece and a gourd quiver

W03

Sunburst Sling

A charged sling whose stones glow brighter the longer they charge

W04

Ember Censer

A fire-god incense burner that throws a short cone of flame

W05

The four Elemental Orbs

Fire, Earth, Wind and Water, with the chest-glow emblems they leave once owned

W06

The Sun Orb

The brightest object in the game, in a cradle of four carved hands

W07

K'in, the spirit wisp

The companion light and its VFX states

W08

Kaya's field kit

Notebooks, rubbing kit, brushes, compass and goggles
