Story

The Short Story

“The Likeness”, an archaeology mystery set in the Sun Temple. Four chapters, in progress.

Kaya dies on her very first trap and wakes at the Threshold to find her own likeness freshly carved into the Spirit Mural, beside a betrayal panel that contradicts what the temple's spirit guide has told her. To keep climbing, she must decide how much of K'in's edited history she is willing to carry.

A contained, atmospheric mystery with the heart of a coming-of-age adventure: the pleasure of watching a scholar read a wall like a crime scene. The mood is warm, curious and a little melancholic, with eerie rather than gory danger.

Four chapters

From the camp to the middle portal

Chapter 1

The Overlooked Find

Kaya arrives at the foot of the pyramid, wakes K'in, and dies in the Shell within the hour. When she wakes at the Threshold, something on the mural is new.

Chapter 2

Reading Between the Layers

She reads the wall like a crime scene: a fresh carving, a panel cut in newer stone, tally marks in a codex margin. Oxlahun is very helpful.

Chapter 3

The Confrontation in the Dust

Kaya brings her questions to K'in in front of the mural. The temple answers before K'in does.

Chapter 4

What the Ground Remembers

Kaya decides what to carry up the stairs, and what to write down in her own hand.

The evidence

Three clues, two witnesses

Kaya has three clues: the carved likeness, the re-cut panel, and the codex open on the lectern, whose margin is marked with tallies that add up to more deaths than she remembers.

And she has two witnesses: a patient, grieving spirit she needs for every trap above her, and Oxlahun, the talkative trader at the offering table, who buys and sells Spirit Fragments and has every reason to want her to keep dying.

The soft antagonist

A friend at the offering table

Friendly, useful and invested in her failure. On a first read Oxlahun's help is help; on a second read it is something else.
