Story

The Story

A scholar's daughter, a temple that records every death, and a guide who has been editing history out of love and grief.

Kaya came to the Sun Temple with a field notebook, a satchel of brushes and charcoal, and one purpose: to prove her mother right.

Her mother, the epigrapher Dr. Isabel Kaan, spent years arguing that the great mural in the temple's base hall records a betrayal the builders deliberately hid. Her colleagues laughed her out of the field. Kaya wakes K'in, the last spirit of the temple, climbs onto the first floor, and dies in a corridor of rising spikes within the hour.

She wakes on the stone floor of the Threshold, exactly where she started, and K'in greets her as though nothing is wrong.

The first death

The temple does not forget

Anyone who dies inside the temple wakes again in the Threshold with their memories intact. Death is not erased, it is recorded: the mural gains a likeness of every climber, and tally marks gather in the margin of an old codex.

Every return is a second chance for the climber, and another entry in the temple's archive.

The likeness

A carving she never made

On the Spirit Mural, in pale limestone still dusted with fresh chips, a new figure has appeared: a slender young woman with twin braids, a satchel on her left hip and goggles pushed up on her forehead.

Beside it stand older carvings of the climbers who came before her: a surveyor with a notched staff, a woman with a coil of rope, a young man with a lantern, a small figure with a satchel much like Kaya's, and one carving that was never finished. Meet the Carved Climbers →

The guide

A spirit who edits history

K'in is gentle, patient and indispensable. She lights the way, warns of traps and pulls Kaya's spirit back after every fall. She also tells the temple's history in short, memorable lines, and the oldest glyphs on the wall do not always agree with her.

Kaya has to decide how much of K'in's account she is willing to carry, and whether she can keep climbing with a guide she cannot fully trust.

K'in's history

One line for every tier

Each tier ends in a Portal Chamber, where a fragment of the mural lights up and K'in speaks one line of the temple's history. Together they tell the official version of what happened. Whether it is the true one is another matter.

  1. Tier IThe Flooded Foundations“The ancients harnessed balance.”
  2. Tier IIThe Trap Galleries“The first element betrayed them.”
  3. Tier IIIThe Shifting Vaults“The temple learned to move, so no thief would find the same path twice.”
  4. Tier IVThe Elemental Sanctums“The orbs scattered, and guardians were born.”
  5. Tier VThe Illusion Depths“The elements turned on one another.”
  6. Tier VIThe Ascension“A single orb chose memory over power.”
  7. SummitThe Sky Altar“The cycle begins again… unless you break it.”

The ending

Restore the cycle, or break it

At the summit, Kaya places the Sun Orb on the altar before the great Sun Portal. Two pedestals flank it: one glowing gold, one deep violet.

Restoring the cycle keeps the temple alive as it is. Breaking it ends the loop for good. What either choice costs depends on what Kaya has learned on the way up.
