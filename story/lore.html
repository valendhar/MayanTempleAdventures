Lore & History

The builders, the four orbs, the betrayal, the curse and the sealing, and why the record cannot be trusted.

The temple's history survives in three versions: the carved Spirit Mural, an old codex on a lectern, and the short lines K'in speaks at each tier. They do not quite agree.

The balance

Four elements, four orbs

The priesthood of the Sun Temple harnessed fire, earth, wind and water through four sacred orbs, and built the temple around the Sun Portal on its summit. Balance among the elements was their highest ideal, and the source of their power.

The betrayal

One element broke the balance

Then one element betrayed the others. The balance shattered, the priests were cursed into undeath and the orbs scattered up through the floors as wards.

K'in's account is simple: “The first element betrayed them.” But one panel of the mural has been re-cut in newer stone with different tool marks, and the older glyphs around it suggest the first element may have been the one betrayed.

The curse

The priests who never left

The cursed priests still walk the temple as Risen Guardians, wrapped like funerary bundles, a cold sigil glowing on each chest. Their high priest waits on the summit as a towering hollow skeleton, four stolen lights circling in his open ribcage.

Whether he caused the betrayal, or only took the blame for it, is one of the questions the climb has to answer. The Risen Guardians →

The sealing

A temple that closed itself

After the catastrophe the pyramid sealed itself and the Sun Portal went dark. K'in, once a priestess, chose to stay.

Since then the temple has rearranged its chambers to trap greedy intruders, and the cycle of death and return has run long enough for the mural to fill with the faces of climbers.

The scholar who was right

Dr. Isabel Kaan's theory

Kaya's mother, an epigrapher, argued that the Spirit Mural records a betrayal the builders deliberately hid. Her peers dismissed the theory, and she has been missing ever since. Her battered notebook travels in Kaya's satchel, next to Kaya's own.

Kaya enters the temple to vindicate her, and has to learn the difference between proving a point and reading what the evidence actually says. Dr. Isabel Kaan →
