Reading the Temple

Kaya's goggles, her chisel, her notebook, and the two versions of every story on the wall.

Everyone else who climbed this temple took the stairs. Kaya reads the wall.

Reading is the game's signature. It's how she survives the fights, how she finds what's hidden, and how the story reaches you. Everything she learns stays with her through every death.

Her mother's goggles

The Lens

The bronze goggles Isabel left behind. Hold a button to lower them, and the world answers.

What you readWhat it gives you
Enemy sigilsMarks a weak point, and each enemy type you read stays in the notebook and gets easier
TrapsShows a timing ring on the trap and its trigger
Floors and wallsShows false floors, hidden paths, cracked walls and secret passages
GlyphsAdds what you see to your lexicon

Two guides

K'in's path, or your own eyes

K'in's ghost path is free, always safe and always there. The Lens costs Focus and is always true.

On the lower floors they agree. Higher up, you may start to notice what her path walks past.

From Floor 6

Recut

A chisel changes how Kaya sees the temple's defences. With the Lens on, she can recut a marked trap's glyph, and for a few seconds it hits only her enemies. The trap makers built everything to answer to the old glyphs, and she can read them.

The wall is evidence

Two readings of every fragment

K'in tells you what a fragment means. The glyphs say what they say. They don't always agree, and the story lives in the difference.

  1. Take a rubbing

    At each Portal Chamber, charcoal and paper over the fragment that just lit up. Once per floor, and yours for good.

  2. Build a lexicon

    Each glyph you've seen has a meaning that's blank, possible or confirmed.

  3. Read it

    At the desk, K'in's version sits on one side. On the other you fill in the glyphs. A wrong guess costs nothing; the sentence just doesn't resolve.

  4. Confirm it

    Isabel's rule: never act on an unverified reading. A meaning is confirmed when two sources agree.

The notebook

Noted, not read

The map, the bestiary, the trap ledger, the lexicon and the journal are all pages of Kaya's field notebook, written in her voice. Every new enemy, trap and death adds a line.

On the back page she keeps a count, in her mother's bar-and-dot numerals. It started at 412.
