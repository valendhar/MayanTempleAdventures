The Game

Weapons & Powers

Four weapons, four permanent Elemental Orbs and the one Sun Orb that opens the summit.

Weapons

An explorer's arsenal, found in the temple

Kaya enters as a scholar, not a warrior. Every weapon is a discovery, and each one changes what she can reach as much as what she can fight.

Melee · found in the Shell

Obsidian Macuahuitl

A flat paddle war-club of dark hardwood edged with black obsidian blades, banded in hammered gold with a jade glyph strip down its centre. Light combo plus a heavy finisher; on a heavy attack the glyphs flare gold. Lifting it from the sword shrine is what wakes the first guardian.

Ranged, fast · found on Floor 2

Spirit Blowgun

A long reed-and-wood tube with a carved jade hummingbird mouthpiece, gold bands and a painted gourd quiver. The first ranged weapon: it strikes glyph targets high on shrine walls and keeps archers honest.

Ranged, charged · from the Threshold after Tier I

Sunburst Sling

Braided cotton cords, a pouch backed by a small gold sun disc and carved river stones that glow brighter the longer the shot is charged. A fully charged stone leaves like a small sun, and cracks armoured husks.

Short flame cone · found with the Fire Orb

Ember Censer

A clay incense burner shaped like the face of an old fire god, swung on a bronze chain. It breathes a short cone of flame, lights braziers and burns away vine-choked doors.

Permanent powers

The four Elemental Orbs

The builders harnessed fire, earth, wind and water through four sacred orbs. Kaya claims them one by one in the Elemental Sanctums, and unlike everything else she gathers, they stay with her spirit through every death.

Each orb is a traversal tool, a combat power and a key. Rooms that need an orb only enter the Trials room pool once she owns it, so the temple literally grows as she does.

The goal

The Sun Orb

The brightest thing in the game. A blazing golden orb with a living sun glyph at its core, cradled in four carved gold hands set with red, green, white and blue gems, one for each element.

It waits in a sealed vault behind the Priest's Lieutenant on Floor 17. Carried to the Sky Altar, it opens the Sun Portal and puts the final choice in Kaya's hands. The summit →

Puzzle energy

Energy Orbs

The working orbs of every puzzle room: gold sun, blue water, red fire and green growth, each held in a gold-and-jade band. They can be carried, socketed and combined: pour sun into water and you get growth.
