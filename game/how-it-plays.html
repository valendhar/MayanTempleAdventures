The Game

How It Plays

Move, read, fight, solve. Then die, return to the Threshold, and climb again a little stronger.

Four nested loops

From one room to the whole temple

  1. Room

    Arrive on the twin pads, traverse and fight, solve the room's lock, and the exit gate opens to the next room.

  2. Floor

    Room A, then Room B with the floor's Key Orb, then the Portal Chamber: socket the orb, hear K'in's line, light a mural fragment, bank your coins and ascend.

  3. Run

    Leave the Threshold and climb. Death pulls Kaya's spirit back: most coins are lost, Spirit Fragments are kept. Or reach the summit.

  4. Meta

    Spend fragments at the Threshold, unlock weapons and gear, fill the Spirit Mural, open tier shortcuts and claim the Elemental Orbs that reshape the climb.

Move

Platforming that never waits

Run, sprint, jump, mantle, crouch and swim, across a platform set that keeps changing: sliding slabs, floating and swinging platforms, seesaws, rotating calendar discs, crumbling floors, vanishing phase steps, updrafts and lifts.

Every room hands Kaya one new idea, then the next room asks her to use it while something else is trying to kill her.

Read

Traps with a rhythm

Spikes, fire vents, fireball serpent heads, saw tracks, pendulums, dart walls, rolling boulders and crushing walls. Each fires on a readable beat, and K'in flickers a warning before it goes off.

Getting through once is luck. Getting through clean means you have learned the beats.

Fight

Cursed priests and awakened stone

Light combos and a heavy finisher with the macuahuitl, then ranged tools for archers on ledges and targets out of reach. Risen Guardians shamble, Husk Archers hold the high ground, Obsidian Panthers flank, Stone Sentries charge, and Guardian Spirits can only be outrun.

Meet the creatures and bosses →

Solve

Orbs, plates, light and memory

Carry energy orbs between pedestals, press plates in the order a mural shows, bounce sunlight off bronze mirrors, split beams through coloured prisms, align animal totems and walk a path that is only visible while K'in sings.

K'in lights the orb that is wrong rather than the one that is right, so the solving is still yours.

Modes

One room pool, three ways to climb

Story

The Ascent

A fixed, hand-built room order. The first playthrough, with the Shell as the tutorial that teaches every core verb once.

Unlocked after the first summit

Trials of the Shifting Temple

Rooms shuffle within each tier while the tier order stays fixed. Stackable Temple's Wrath curses add tougher undead, faster traps, fewer checkpoints, roaming Guardian Spirits and timed floors, and each level earns better rewards.

Replay

Expedition

Return to any cleared floor in its story layout with your full kit, to collect idols, earn medallions and open the orb-gated side routes.

Collectibles and economy

What Kaya carries out of every room

Energy Orbs

In-room puzzle currency in four colours. Sun poured into water makes growth.

Key Orb

One per floor. Socketing it opens the Portal Chamber's great door.

Golden Idols

Exactly one hidden in every room, for lore and completion.

Coins and Spirit Fragments

Coins are spent during a run and mostly lost on death. Fragments are what the spirit keeps.

Codex pages, scrolls and medallions

Painted map pages, lore scrolls, glyph echoes, ancient shards for cosmetics, and trial medallions for speed, no damage, all coins and deathless runs.
