The Game

How It Plays

Read, fight, move, solve. Then die, return to the Threshold, and climb again knowing more.

Four nested loops

From one chamber to the whole temple

  1. Chamber

    45 to 120 seconds. The door seals. Fight, cross, or both. Then choose the next gate by the reward carved on it.

  2. Floor

    Two to four fights around the floor's authored rooms, then the Key Orb, then the Portal Chamber: take a rubbing, ring Oxlahun's bell, and decide whether to climb on.

  3. Run

    25 to 45 minutes from a lit Sun Gate, until you die or walk home. Your health carries over from room to room, and healing is rare.

  4. Return

    Wake at the Threshold. Sell or study what you carried, spend what you earned, and go back. The Return

Read

The goggles change what you see

Hold a button and Kaya lowers her mother's bronze goggles. Time slows for a moment. Enemy weak points, trap timings, false floors and hidden cracks show themselves.

It runs on Focus, which you earn by fighting well. Reading the Temple

Fight

Up to twelve at once

Light combos, a heavy finisher, ranged tools and a quick dodge with a moment of invulnerability. Risen Guardians shamble, Bearers block, archers hold the high ground, panthers flank, Stone Sentries charge, and Guardian Spirits can only be outrun.

Meet the creatures and bosses

Use the room

Traps hurt everyone

Spikes, saws, pendulums, dart walls, fire vents, crushers and boulders fire on a beat you can learn, and they do triple damage to enemies. Luring a wave into a saw is often better than fighting it.

On Floor 6 Kaya finds a chisel. From then on she can recut a trap's glyph, so that for a few seconds it hits only her enemies.

Move

Platforming between the fights

Run, sprint, jump, mantle and swim, across sliding slabs, swinging platforms, seesaws, rotating calendar discs, crumbling floors, phase steps, updrafts and lifts.

A missed jump costs a little health and puts you back on the last safe ledge. It never costs the room.

Solve

Puzzles that come back changed

Carry energy orbs, press plates in a mural's order, bounce sunlight off mirrors, split beams through prisms, sing with the song stones, align animal totems.

The first solve is a study. After that, the room is Known: the temple reseeds the answer, your notebook keeps the method, and the second solve takes thirty seconds with something trying to kill you.

Carved gates

Every exit is a choice

Most chambers end in two or three gates, each with a glowing glyph that shows what lies behind it.

GlyphWhat it gives you
Day sign stoneA blessing from that sign. The main way to build power in a run.
Coin bowlCoins to spend at the next Waystall or shrine.
SatchelA cache of Finds to carry out and sell.
Gold shardSpirit Fragments, which you always keep.
Honey jarMore maximum health. Rare.
ChiselA graving that changes your weapon for this run.
Open bookA codex or lore page. Permanent.
Water basinA Spirit Shrine: heal and shop, no fight.
Red sigilAn elite chamber. Harder, and worth double.

Blessings

Five day signs, a new build every run

Blessings change your strike, heavy attack, shot, dodge and goggles until you die. Two signs can combine into something rarer.

After the summit

Two more portals

Left portal

Expedition

Return to any cleared floor at low danger with your full kit, to open orb-gated side routes and collect idols and evidence.

Right portal

Trials of the Shifting Temple

Opens after the first summit. Choose curses from Temple's Wrath before a run: more undead, faster traps, a hunting spirit, a silent K'in. The higher the Wrath, the richer the reward.

Every chamber

Your own ghost

Your best time through any chamber walks ahead of you as a faint golden figure, if you want something to race.

Collectibles

What Kaya carries out

Energy Orbs

Puzzle energy in the temple's colours. Sun poured into water makes growth.

Key Orb

One per floor. Socketing it opens the Portal Chamber's great door.

Golden Idols

One hidden in every authored room, off the safe path. Worth a good price, and a line of lore.

Coins and Spirit Fragments

Coins die with you. Fragments are what the spirit keeps.

Codex pages and scrolls

Evidence, and the most valuable things you can carry. Oxlahun agrees, which is interesting.
