The Game

Climbers & Co-op

You play yourself, the temple carves you into its wall, and up to four Climbers go up together.

You are a Climber. You play your own Fortnite character, and K'in greets you the way she greets everyone the temple lets in: climbing soul.

The temple has always kept a record of the people it takes. The Carved Climbers on the Spirit Mural came before you. You are next.

Your likeness

Carved into the wall

The first time a Climber dies, a new panel is cut into the Spirit Mural: a carved figure, your name in a glyph frame, and a sigil you choose from the four totem animals, jaguar, serpent, eagle or monkey.

The panel grows with you. Every tier you clear adds a band to it, and the ending you reach can gild it. Everyone in the Threshold sees everyone else's panel, so the wall becomes a record of who has climbed how far.

Kaya in the game

Beside you first, then at the wall

Kaya is no longer the one you play. She is the one you climb with, and then the one you climb for.

  1. Floor 0

    Kaya climbs the Shell beside the party, waits for you at every section and points the way. In the spike corridor the temple takes her, and she dissolves into gold light.

  2. The Threshold

    You find her back at the Threshold, alive, staring at her own fresh carving on the Spirit Mural. From then on she stays to read the wall, and sends Climbers up for the evidence she needs: mural fragments, codex pages, her mother's notes.

  3. On the way up

    Three times on the climb, Kaya meets the party with something new she has read in the stone. What she finds is for the players to discover.

Up to four

How a party climbs

Every room can still be solved alone. Playing together only adds speed, or a second way through.

At the exit gate

No one left behind

A room's exit opens when its lock is solved and every living Climber stands at the gate.

Per floor

One Key Orb

The party carries a single Key Orb to the Portal Chamber. If its carrier falls, anyone can pick it up.

When you fall

Back on your feet

In the story, a fallen Climber returns at the room's checkpoint after a few seconds, as long as someone in the party is still standing.

Orbs you lack

Borrowed echoes

If a room needs an Elemental Orb a friend has not claimed yet, K'in lends them a glowing echo of it for that room only.

Between sessions

Catch up together

Progress belongs to each player. Friends who are behind still earn everything, and get the floor credited when the party clears it.

On the summit

The ending vote

Each Climber stands at Restore or Break. The majority decides, and every player's own save remembers how they voted.
