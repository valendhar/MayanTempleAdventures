The Game

Overview & Pillars

A third-person action-adventure platformer inside a living Mayan pyramid, planned for Unreal Engine 5.

Kaya, a young explorer, wakes K'in, the last spirit of an ancient Mayan sun temple, at its threshold. The temple is alive: it rearranges itself and swallows everyone who climbs it.

Floor by floor she fights the risen guardians, survives the traps, solves the orb puzzles and earns the elemental powers the builders once held, until she carries the Sun Orb to the great portal on the summit and decides the temple's fate: restore the cycle, or break it.

The story explains the structure. The temple “rearranges itself to trap greedy adventurers”, so shuffled room layouts belong to the world rather than to a menu. And “the cycle begins again… unless you break it”: when Kaya dies, K'in pulls her spirit back to the Threshold. What her spirit keeps between cycles, the Elemental Orbs and the fragments she has gathered, is what makes the next climb go further.

Genre
Third-person action-adventure platformer with a roguelite climb
Planned engine
Unreal Engine 5
Inspirations
Ratchet & Clank and Jak & Daxter for tone; Hades for the return-and-grow loop
Players
Solo first. Every room has two arrival pads and wide corridors for 2-player co-op
Campaign
Floor 0, seventeen floors in six tiers, and the Sky Altar summit
Setting
The interior of one living stepped pyramid, plus the jungle forecourt and the open summit

Design pillars

Five ideas every room has to serve

  1. 01

    Ascend

    Every run is a climb. The temple beneath you is progress made visible: the hub sits at the foot of a real pyramid, and the Sky Altar you are climbing toward is its actual summit.

  2. 02

    Fight

    Melee and ranged combat against cursed priests and awakened creatures. The obsidian macuahuitl comes first, then the blowgun, the sling and the ember censer.

  3. 03

    Solve

    Puzzles grow with the climb: one mechanic per room in the lower tiers, combined mechanics in the middle, layered perception puzzles under pressure near the top.

  4. 04

    Grow for good

    The four Elemental Orbs and the upgrades bought in the Threshold stay with Kaya between runs, and each one opens routes that were closed before.

  5. 05

    Never alone

    K'in teaches, warns, foreshadows and remembers. She draws ghost paths after a missed jump, flickers before a trap fires and greets every return.

First steps

The jungle forecourt

Every climb begins at Kaya's camp at the foot of the pyramid: a canvas tent, a kettle on the fire, two straw training dummies and a carved target to practise on. Ahead, between two great feathered-serpent heads, the low tunnel leads into the Threshold.

The pyramid is always in view, so the summit is a goal you can see from the very first minute.

Look and feel

Colourful, playful, grounded

Painterly and stylized in the register of Ratchet & Clank and Jak & Daxter, but believable: carved limestone, jade inlay, hammered gold, glowing glyphs, mossy weathered stone, obsidian and torch flame.

Four energy colours mark every interactive object: gold for sun, blue for water, red for fire and green for growth. More on the art direction →
